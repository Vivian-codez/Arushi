Subject: Debates I Will Probably Keep Losing

Miss Arushi,

We haven’t known each other for more than a month. And honestly speaking, it’s been long enough since I properly saw you that I’ve almost forgotten what you look like. Most of what I know about you is pieced together from random facts and glimpses of your personality. But the parts I do know? I genuinely like them. And I’d love nothing more than to discover the rest.

I know you carry fierce ambitions, and on paper, our paths don’t neatly align. We aren’t the textbook definition of compatible—you have NEET, and I have placements and internships to chase. I know I’m probably far from your ideal type. You bring chaos to my calm. You lean avoidant, and I tend to be anxious, an impossible combination.

Yet despite all of that, I find myself drawn to you. I love our debates, even though nine times out of ten I end up losing them. I like the moments when we talk about the deep, vulnerable things. Half the time, the only reason I open Instagram is just to see if you texted. And if you asked me what I like most about you, it’s the person you bring out in me when your chaos meets my world. I admire your intelligence, the way you always have something witty to say, your godly taste in music, and even how particular you are about the things you love. I’m still learning your likes and pet peeves and still figuring out how to actually make things right whenever I mess up.

I won’t pretend I have it all together. I am definitely not like the girl you like from Ladakh, nor like anyone else you cross paths with online. I can get protective, jealous, and unsure. I catch feelings quickly, but I hold onto them wholeheartedly as long as the other person values and reciprocates them. I am still a work in progress, learning and unlearning, trying to become the best version of myself.

I know how sacred your dreams are to you, and I never want to be someone who stands in the way of what you want to achieve. Instead, I want to be right there on the sidelines,quietly cheering you on and having your back while you work on your goals and I build my own future.

I know you don't really believe in long-distance, but perhaps for now, distance is just a quiet space for us to build something real until we can finally stand face-to-face. I’m not asking for all your hours or impossible promises. I just want one small, sincere commitment to try. Whether this works or not, one thing is certain: I will always respect you, your space, and your boundaries.

— Vivian
